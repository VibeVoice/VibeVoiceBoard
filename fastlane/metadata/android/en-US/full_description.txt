Stop typing. Start speaking.

VibeVoiceBoard is a full Android keyboard with dictation built into it. Tap the mark in the toolbar or hold the space bar, say what you mean, and the text appears in whatever field you were already in — a message, an email, a document, a commit message. It is a keyboard first, so everything else about typing keeps working exactly as before.

<b>It writes the punctuation for you</b>
Commas, full stops, question marks and sentence breaks are applied from how you speak, not from saying the word "comma". Casing too. A dictated paragraph comes out looking like a written one.

<b>Measured accuracy, published method</b>
9.31% word error rate, averaged across twelve fixtures in six languages. That figure, the fixtures behind it and the way it is scored are all written up at vibevoice.net — including the one fixture we keep because dropping it would improve the number.

<b>Faster than your thumbs</b>
People type at around 40 words a minute and speak at around 150. In the closest thing to a controlled measurement anybody has published (Ruan et al., ACM IMWUT 2017), speech ran at 153 words a minute against 52 typed. Roughly three times, on a phone keyboard, for anything longer than a line.

<b>Over fifty languages</b>
Recognition is not tied to your keyboard layout, and you do not have to tell it which language you are speaking.

<b>It keeps going when the keyboard closes</b>
Switch this on and a dictation survives leaving the app. Start a thought in one place and finish it in another; a notification shows what has been heard so far and ends the session. Optionally a small floating mark stays on screen, pulsing with your voice, which you drag onto an X to stop.

<b>Voice typing needs a VibeVoice account</b>
Transcription runs on the VibeVoice service, not on your device. The free tier is 30 minutes a month at no cost; Pro is €3 a month for 180 minutes and Ultra €10 for 6000. You create the account in a browser and link it once — the app shows you a code and waits.

Everything else in the keyboard works with no account and no network at all.

<b>What leaves the device</b>
Audio is streamed to vibevoice.net only while a session is running — while you hold the space bar, or after you tap the mark, with the keyboard visibly recording. Nothing is transmitted at any other time. Recordings are deleted as soon as the transcript exists or the attempt fails.

If you send a bug report from the settings, the diagnostic log goes with it, and that log can contain text you dictated earlier. The privacy policy at vibevoice.net covers what is processed and how long it is kept.

<b>A real keyboard underneath</b>
VibeVoiceBoard is built on HeliBoard, itself derived from AOSP and OpenBoard, and it keeps what that gives you: multilingual typing with per-language layouts, glide typing, clipboard history, one-handed and split modes, a customisable toolbar, adjustable key height and spacing, themes with your own colours, and a layout editor. No ads, no tracking, and no account needed for any of it.
